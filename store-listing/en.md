# Manage Instagram Followers

Find out who does not follow you back and safely clean up your Instagram connections.

Manage Instagram Followers works directly inside Instagram and helps you:

- Find accounts that do not follow you back.
- See mutual connections.
- Find followers you have not followed back.
- Search and sort accounts by name, username, or relationship.
- Follow back, unfollow, or cancel pending requests.
- Select non-followers and process them one at a time with safety limits.
- Use the interface in English or Portuguese.

Your Instagram credentials and connection data are never sent to the developer. Actions go directly from your browser to Instagram and are verified before the interface changes.
